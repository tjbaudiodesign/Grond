# Changelog

## 2.1.0
- Tells an Anvil expander on its right when a fire starts and when everything it triggered has finished (end of cycle), so Anvil can capture each hit.

## 2.0.0
- First release.
- Two sample banks (10 impact + 10 whoosh slots) with per-bank layering, randomisation, timing/align and debris.
- Doppler for whooshes, with auto-centre on the loudest part of the sample.
- Built-in Disseminator (disperser), Distortion, Spectral Delay and Multiband (OTT-style) effects.
- CV inputs for timing, distortion and spectral delay parameters.
